FAQ

Things you're probably wondering

Asked by business owners before they got started.

How is this different from SEO?
Completely different channel, different signals, different results. Traditional SEO puts you on Google's blue links — a channel losing traffic every quarter. GEO gets you seen by AI — where customers are going first. The signals AI engines care about are brand mentions, structured data, and citability — not backlinks and keywords. We do both, but GEO is where the growth is going.
How long before I see results?
Technical fixes like schema markup, robots.txt, and llms.txt can move your GEO score within two to four weeks. Brand authority and citability improvements take two to three months to compound. We track your score every month so you see exactly what is moving and what is not.
Do I need to change my whole website?
No. Most GEO fixes are additive — we add structured data, create an llms.txt file, update robots.txt, and rewrite specific content blocks. Your existing site stays intact. We work within your current CMS and only touch the pages and files that need updating.
I already have an SEO agency. Will this conflict?
Not at all — we work alongside your current agency, not against them. Your SEO agency owns Google. SeenBy owns AI search. They're separate channels. Most of our clients run both. The question is: are you being seen on the channel that's growing 527% year over year? Your SEO agency isn't doing that.
What businesses is this for?
Any business where customers search for recommendations online. F&B, professional services, retail, property, healthcare, SaaS, e-commerce. If someone in your city might ask ChatGPT or Perplexity for a recommendation in your category, you need GEO. The audit will tell you exactly how visible — how seen — you currently are.
What happens after the audit?
You receive two PDF reports — one covering your full marketing health, one covering your GEO visibility score. We walk through the findings on a 45-minute call. You will know exactly what is broken, what to fix first, and what the projected impact is. There is no pressure to continue. Most clients do.
